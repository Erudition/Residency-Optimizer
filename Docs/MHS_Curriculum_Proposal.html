Proposed MHS Internal Medicine Curriculum (4+1 Model)

This schedule reflects an X+Y (4+1) alternating block model. In this structure, the academic year consists of ten 5-week cycles per year. For each cycle, residents spend 4 weeks on a core rotation, followed immediately by 1 week dedicated entirely to continuity clinic. * Total Cycle: 10 core blocks (40 weeks) + 10 clinic weeks (10 weeks) + 2 weeks standard vacation/holiday = 52 weeks. * Continuity Structure: For the 2026-2027 academic year, the PGY-2 class will complete their "+1" continuity clinic blocks at NIMA, remaining at this site to finish out their residency. The PGY-1 and current PGY-3 classes are assigned to CCIM for their longitudinal continuity site.

In addition to the 5th-week clinic, residents will cycle through dedicated month-long (4-week) ambulatory rotations (such as completing a month in the NIMA clinic) to satisfy ACGME general outpatient requirements and provide staffing support.

Jeopardy & Backup Coverage

To maintain the required 4+1 structure and preserve inpatient caps without compromising continuity clinics or mandatory subspecialty blocks, Jeopardy coverage is strictly defined to utilize residents on flexible time, guaranteeing continuous year-round availability. Interns are explicitly protected from Jeopardy pulls. * Jeopardy Hierarchy: If an intern calls out, Attendings or Seniors will pick up the patient volume directly. * 1st Line (PGY-3): Handled by a PGY-3 currently assigned to a flexible Elective or easy Consult block. * 2nd Line (PGY-2): If no PGY-3 is available, a PGY-2 on an easy Elective or Consult block will be pulled. * Final Line (Chiefs): Chief residents serve as the absolute last line of backup coverage.

Leave & Vacation Policy

To ensure compliance with the program's explicit Time Away Request rules and protect the 4+1 patient continuity rhythm, the scheduling engine and block assignments must reflect the following constraints: * Rotation Restrictions: PTO/Vacation is strictly PROHIBITED during Wards, ICU, and the "+1" CCIM/NIMA continuity clinic blocks. Vacations must be requested and mapped exclusively out of flexible Elective or Consult blocks. * Blackout Dates: No PTO is permitted during program transition periods: June 22 - July 10 and August 10 - 31. * Structure: Vacation time must be taken on consecutive days bridged to a weekend (weekends are counted as regular days off, not PTO). Coverage requests require minimum 60-day notice. * Leaves of Absence (Medical, Parental, & Caregiver Leave): In accordance with ABIM and ACGME guidelines, the program supports necessary parental, medical, and caregiver leave. The 4+1 curriculum is structurally designed to help absorb standard extended leaves by remapping them over your designated Elective blocks, minimizing the risk to core clinical minimums. However, individual scheduling needs and potential impacts on graduation timelines will be addressed on a case-by-case basis with program leadership.

Schedule Adjustments for Required Rotations (Deficit Recovery)

If a resident takes PTO or extended leave during a mandated rotation (e.g., Mandatory Geriatrics, Split Neurology), the scheduling engine will enforce the following deficit recovery rules: * The Competency Override (Standard 1-Week PTO): If a resident takes 1 week of PTO during a 4-week mandatory block, the system does not force a makeup. Completing 3 of 4 weeks satisfies competency exposure minimums. * The Minimum Threshold Flag (Short Blocks): If PTO reduces a 2-week mandatory split block (e.g., Pulmonology) to 1 week or less, the system triggers a Subspecialty Deficit Flag. The algorithm will then identify the resident's next "Pure Elective" block and automatically overwrite the necessary weeks to recuperate the mandated exposure. * Leave of Absence Re-calc: During extended leave (e.g., parental/medical), the engine removes scheduled Wards/ICU blocks. Upon return, it cannibalizes subsequent pure elective blocks to reinstate any missed ACGME mandatory minimums. If insufficient elective time remains, the resident is flagged for a potential Training Extension.

ACGME Balance Audit

  • Total Wards + ICU: ~ 14 to 15 months [Min Required: 10]
  • Total ICU: 4 months exact (PGY1 = 2, PGY2 = 1, PGY3 = 1). [Min 2, Max 6, evenly spread, avoiding PGY-1 exclusivity]
  • Dedicated Outpatient Blocks: 10 continuity weeks per year plus 1 block at NIMA [Meets Min 10 months requirement]
  • Night Float: 2 weeks per year in PGY1, PGY2, and PGY3 [Meets max 2 per year / 4 total limits]
  • Subspecialties: All 9 IM ABIM subspecialties can be represented easily through 4-week and 2-week split blocks.
  • Mandatory Multi-disciplinary: Geriatrics, Palliative, Addiction, EM, Neurology explicitly scheduled. EM uses 4 weeks exactly.
