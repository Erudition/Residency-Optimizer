# Simplified ACGME Internal Medicine Requirements

This document serves as a quick-reference guide for the core ACGME Program Requirements for Graduate Medical Education in Internal Medicine, specifically focusing on clinical experiences, structural time minimums, and mandatory rotations.

## 1. Structural Time Allocations (Over 3 Years)
To successfully graduate, residents must meet specific temporal benchmarks across their 36-month training period.

*   **Total Clinical Experiences:** Minimum of **30 months** of primarily clinical experiences.
*   **Inpatient and Critical Care:** Minimum of **10 months** combined.
*   **Outpatient/Ambulatory:** Minimum of **10 months** of foundational ambulatory experiences (approx. 33% of the 30-month clinical minimum).
*   **Individualized Experiences:** At least **6 months** directed toward the resident's future practice (e.g., pure electives, fellowship tracks, or dedicated research).

## 2. Critical Care Specifics
The intensive care unit (ICU) requirements are strictly capped to ensure a balanced, generalist education.

*   **Minimum Duration:** At least **2 months** of critical care must be completed.
*   **Maximum Cap:** Residents can complete **no more than 6 months** of critical care overall. *(This cap is why renaming specialized consult services—like AMCS/ECMO Consults—is critical).*
*   **Longitudinal Spread:** Critical care exposure must not occur *solely* during the PGY-1 year; it must be distributed into the supervisory PGY-2 and/or PGY-3 years.

## 3. The "Big 9" Core Subspecialties
The program must provide and ensure residents rotate through clinical experiences that cover all nine core ABIM medical subspecialties:

1.  **Cardiology**
2.  **Endocrinology**
3.  **Gastroenterology**
4.  **Hematology**
5.  **Medical Oncology** *(often paired as Heme-Onc)*
6.  **Infectious Disease**
7.  **Nephrology**
8.  **Pulmonology**
9.  **Rheumatology**

> [!NOTE]
> While rigid 1-month requirements per subspecialty are no longer universally enforced by hours, programs must demonstrate meaningful clinical exposure—typically modeled as minimum 2-week contiguous blocks across the residency.

## 4. Mandatory Multidisciplinary Clinical Experiences
In addition to standard internal medicine subspecialties, residents are required to complete dedicated, cross-disciplinary rotations in the following five domains:

1.  **Geriatric Medicine**
2.  **Hospice and Palliative Medicine**
3.  **Addiction Medicine**
4.  **Emergency Medicine** *(Strictly limited to PGY-2 and PGY-3 years; ACGME explicitly restricts interns from primary EM assignments).*
5.  **Neurology**
